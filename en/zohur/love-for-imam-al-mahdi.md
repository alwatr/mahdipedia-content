The Duties of the Shi’a During the Occultation

A Shi’a Muslim should make every possible effort to cultivate love for Imam al-Mahdi, peace be upon him.

There is no doubt that loving all the Infallible Imams, peace be upon them all, is obligatory, that their love is an integral part of faith and a condition for the acceptance of deeds, and that numerous narrations have been transmitted on this subject. However, devotion to our master, the Proof of Allah, Imam al-Mahdi, peace be upon him, possesses a particular significance that has led to a specific emphasis on loving him. This can be understood from two perspectives:

First: Reason

Human nature is inherently inclined to love those who show kindness to it and those through whom kindness and benevolence reach it.

In this regard, a narration attributed to the interpretation transmitted from the Imam, peace be upon him, states that Almighty Allah revealed to Moses, peace be upon him:

“Make Me beloved to My creation, and make My creation beloved to Me.”

Moses asked, “My Lord, how should I do this?”

Allah replied, “Remind them of My blessings and favors so that they may love Me.”

In another narration, cited in Dar al-Salam from Qisas al-Anbiya, the Prophet Muhammad, peace and blessings be upon him and his family, is reported to have said that Allah, the Almighty, revealed to David, peace be upon him:

“Love Me, and make Me beloved to My creation.”

David said, “My Lord, I love You. How can I make You beloved to Your creation?”

Allah replied, “Remind them of My blessings upon them, for when you remind them of these blessings, they will love Me.”

It is also narrated in Majalis al-Saduq, on the authority of Ibn Abbas, that the Messenger of Allah, peace and blessings be upon him and his family, said:

“Love Allah for the blessings He bestows upon you; love me for the love of Allah, the Almighty; and love my Ahl al-Bayt because of your love for me.”

When a person recognizes the kindness and beneficence of our master, Imam al-Hujjah, peace be upon him, understands the rights he has over us, and realizes that all the abundant blessings and countless gifts bestowed upon us by Allah are received through the blessing and mediation of our master, peace be upon him, reason dictates that we should love him. Indeed, our very nature is predisposed to love him.

Second: Scriptural Narrations

Sayyid al-Muhaddith al-Bahrani, may Allah have mercy on him, relates in his book Ghayat al-Maram [1], citing al-Nu’mani through his chain of transmission, a narration from the Messenger of Allah, peace and blessings be upon him and his family, who said:

“On the Night of the Ascension, Allah revealed to me: ‘O Muhammad, whom have you appointed as your successor on earth over your community?’ Although He already knew the answer.

“I replied, ‘My brother.’

“He said, ‘Ali ibn Abi Talib?’

“I replied, ‘Yes, my Lord.’

“He said, ‘O Muhammad, I looked upon the earth and chose you from among its people. I am not mentioned without you also being mentioned with Me. I am the Praised One, and you are Muhammad. Then I looked upon it once more and chose Ali ibn Abi Talib from among its people, appointing him as your successor. You are the master of the prophets, and Ali is the master of the successors. I gave him a name derived from My own names: I am al-A’la, the Most High, and he is Ali, the Exalted.

“‘O Muhammad, if one of My servants were to worship Me until he perished, but then meet Me while denying your authority and divinely ordained guardianship, I would admit him to Hell.’

“Then He said, ‘O Muhammad, would you like to see them?’

“I replied, ‘Yes.’

“He said, ‘Step forward.’

“When I stepped forward, I saw Ali ibn Abi Talib, al-Hasan ibn Ali, al-Husayn ibn Ali, Ali ibn al-Husayn, Muhammad ibn Ali, Ja’far ibn Muhammad, Musa ibn Ja’far, Ali ibn Musa, Muhammad ibn Ali, Ali ibn Muhammad, al-Hasan ibn Ali, and al-Hujjah al-Qa’im, peace be upon them all. He shone among them like a brilliant star.

This narration indicates that love for Imam al-Mahdi, peace be upon him, possesses a particular significance that warranted a specific command from Almighty Allah, even though love for all the Imams, peace be upon them, is obligatory. Several reasons underlie this distinction, including the following:

1. Love for and knowledge of Imam al-Mahdi, peace be upon him, are inseparable from love for and knowledge of the other Imams, peace be upon them; however, the reverse is not necessarily true.

A person may know and love the other Imams without knowing or loving Imam al-Mahdi, peace be upon him.

Therefore, knowing this great Imam and loving him contributes to the completion of a person’s faith.

Supporting this point is a narration cited in Volume 9 of Bihar al-Anwar [2], quoting the book Al-Fada’il, from Imam al-Rida, peace be upon him, through his forefathers, from the Messenger of Allah, peace and blessings be upon him and his family. In this narration, after mentioning the names of the Imams, the Prophet states:

“Whoever wishes to meet Allah with complete faith and sound Islam must acknowledge the divinely ordained authority of the awaited Proof, the Master of the Age. For they are lamps amid darkness, Imams of guidance, and standards of piety. Whoever loves them and acknowledges their authority…”
